COMMON QUESTIONS

Will tile removal damage my subfloor? +
Not if it’s done right — that’s the whole point of hiring it out. We work the tile off without gouging the substrate underneath, so you’re not paying for subfloor repair on top of demo.
Can you remove tile in just one room? +
Yes — bathroom, kitchen, or a single room is the most common call. Full-home tile removal is just as straightforward, just a longer day.
How messy is tile demo? +
Dusty, but contained. We work the area methodically and haul debris out same-day rather than letting it pile up.
